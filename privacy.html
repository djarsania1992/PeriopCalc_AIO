Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Perioperative Risk Calculator (the "Application"), a community-use web application developed by DJ Jarsania, MD ("we," "us," or "our").

We built this Application to serve the community, and we believe your privacy should be absolute. Because of this commitment, this Application does not collect, store, track, or share any personal user data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect zero personal information.

  • No Accounts: You do not need to log in or provide an email address or name to use the Application.
  • No Server Storage: Any data or inputs you enter while using the Application are processed entirely locally in your web browser.
  • No Analytics: We do not use tracking cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party web analytics services.

2. Infrastructure & Hosting Data

Because this Application is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may automatically collect technical logging data from your browser (such as your IP address, web request, and the date and time of your visit). This standard operational data is used by GitHub solely to maintain security, analyze platform performance, and prevent abuse. It is never shared with us, and we do not use it to identify you.

For more information, you can review the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Third-Party Links

Our Application may contain links to external community resources. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. We do not operate these external sites and strongly advise you to review their respective privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, this Application is completely safe for use by individuals of all ages, including children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain transparency. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
