Risk Calculators Overview

RCRI (Revised Cardiac Risk Index)

Clinical use: Predicts major adverse cardiac events (MACE) in non-cardiac surgery.

Interpretation:

  • Score range: 0–6 points
  • 0 points (≈0.4%): Low risk
  • 1 point (≈0.9%): Mildly increased risk
  • 2 points (≈6.6%): Moderate risk
  • ≥3 points (≈11%): High risk

Gupta MICA (Myocardial Infarction or Cardiac Arrest)

Clinical use: Estimates 30-day risk of myocardial infarction or cardiac arrest.

Interpretation:

  • <1%: Low risk
  • 1–5%: Moderate risk
  • >5%: Higher risk

DASI (Duke Activity Status Index)

Clinical use: Estimates functional capacity (METs) from patient-reported activities.

Interpretation:

  • ≥7 METs: Excellent functional capacity; generally low perioperative cardiac risk
  • 4–7 METs: Moderate functional capacity; intermediate risk
  • <4 METs: Poor functional capacity; higher perioperative cardiac risk

ARISCAT

Clinical use: Predicts postoperative pulmonary complications (PPC).

Interpretation:

  • <26 points: Low risk (<5% PPC)
  • 26–44 points: Intermediate risk (~10–15% PPC)
  • ≥45 points: High risk (>30% PPC)

STOP-BANG

Clinical use: Screening tool for obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) risk.

Interpretation:

  • 0–2/8: Low OSA risk
  • 3–4/8: Intermediate OSA risk
  • ≥5/8: High OSA risk

Caprini Score

Clinical use: VTE risk stratification to guide thromboprophylaxis.

Interpretation:

  • 0 points: Low VTE risk
  • 1 point: Moderate VTE risk
  • 2–4 points: High VTE risk
  • ≥5 points: Highest VTE risk

Features

  • Real-time calculation: scores update as you enter data.
  • No default values: each assessment starts with a clean slate.
  • Responsive design: works on desktop and mobile devices.
  • Educational: quick reference interpretations accompany scores.
